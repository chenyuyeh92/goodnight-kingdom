## English

**Name:** Goodnight Kingdom

**Subtitle (30 chars max):** Lull sleepless monsters to bed

**Short description (80 chars max):**
The monsters can't sleep! Lull them to bed across 10 worlds in a cozy shooter.

**Full description:**

Every monster in the Goodnight Kingdom suddenly can't sleep!
They're so noisy that nobody in the kingdom can sleep either. Two sleepy guards rub their eyes and set off on a mission: lull every monster to sleep, one by one.

🌙 Ten worlds, thirty levels
Start in the Insomnia Forest, then journey through desert pyramids, an undersea kingdom, frozen ice fields, a volcano island, the rainforest, the savanna, the mountain clouds and deep mines, all the way to the Moon Castle. Each world has a day, dusk and night level, and its own music.

🫧 Don't defeat them. Tuck them in.
Yawn bubbles, a sheep-counting gun, a pillow cannon, a lullaby harp, a wishing-star wand and more put monsters to sleep. Get close and swing your pillow! Chests in each level hold 10 special weapons, and finding the same one again makes it stronger.

📖 An animated picture-book story
Who stole everyone's sleep? The opening and ending unfold page by page like a moving picture book. Reach the end to find out.

🎨 Draw your own guard
Draw your own guard with a finger or mouse, take it on your adventure, and save the picture to share.

👫 Play together
Two players on one computer. Touch controls on phones and tablets.

✨ Made for families
No ads, no personal data collected, no account needed. Progress stays on your device. Available in English and Chinese.

**Keywords:** shooter, side-scroller, platformer, cozy, cute, family, co-op, picture book, bedtime, monsters

**Rating notes:** Mild cartoon fantasy action (monsters are lulled to sleep, not killed; no blood). No ads, no chat or social features, no data collection.
