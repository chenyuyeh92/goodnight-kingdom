「晚安王國」隱私權政策

生效日期:2026 年 10 月 24 日

「晚安王國」(以下稱「本遊戲」)是在瀏覽器裡玩的 2D 射擊遊戲,由葉宸佑製作。本遊戲從設計開始就不收集任何個人資料。

我們不收集的資料

  • 本遊戲不需要註冊或登入,不會問你的姓名、生日、email、電話、照片或位置。
  • 本遊戲沒有廣告,也沒有使用任何分析、追蹤或廣告工具,不使用 cookie。
  • 本遊戲不會把你的遊戲資料傳送到我們或其他人的伺服器(網站主機的一般紀錄、線上雙人見下方)。

存在你裝置裡的資料

為了讓你下次打開可以接著玩,本遊戲會用瀏覽器的本機儲存空間(localStorage),在你自己的裝置裡存下:背包裡的武器、通關紀錄與最佳時間、看過哪些故事、你畫的守衛圖片與名字、音量與語言設定。這些資料只存在這個瀏覽器裡,我們看不到,也不會傳出去。在遊戲的「設定 → 清除所有存檔」可以刪掉背包、通關紀錄、看過的故事和畫的守衛;清除瀏覽器的網站資料會刪掉全部(包括音量與語言設定)。

網站主機

本遊戲放在 GitHub Pages。和瀏覽任何網站一樣,你的瀏覽器向 GitHub 下載遊戲時,GitHub 為了網站安全,可能會記錄來訪者的 IP 位址,適用 GitHub 的隱私權聲明。我們看不到這些紀錄。

線上雙人(兩台裝置連線)

只有在你選「雙人遊戲 → 開房間/加入房間」時才會用到網路連線:開房間那台的遊戲畫面、聲音會直接傳到加入那台,加入那台的按鍵操作會傳回開房間那台。為了讓兩台裝置找到彼此,會透過 Supabase 的即時訊息服務交換房間號碼與連線資訊,並用公開的 STUN 服務(Cloudflare、Google)查詢裝置的網路位址;這些服務和一起玩的另一台裝置會看到你的 IP 位址。傳送的內容不含姓名或任何個人資料,不錄影、不儲存,離開房間後就結束。

你畫的守衛

「畫你的守衛」畫的圖只存在你的裝置裡,不會上傳。按「下載圖片」時,圖片由你的裝置存下或交給你選的分享方式處理;之後怎麼分享由你決定。

外部連結

本遊戲只連到本網站的支援、隱私權與授權頁面。

兒童隱私

本遊戲適合全年齡遊玩。因為我們不收集任何個人資料,所以沒有需要家長同意收集或刪除的資料。

政策更新

如果之後本政策有修改,我們會更新這一頁,並修改上面的生效日期。

Goodnight Kingdom Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Goodnight Kingdom (the "Game") is a 2D shooter played in the web browser, made by Chen-Yu Yeh (葉宸佑). The Game is designed to collect no personal information at all.

What we do not collect

  • No sign-up or login. The Game never asks for a name, birthday, email, phone number, photo or location.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking or advertising tools, and no cookies.
  • The Game does not send your game data to our servers or anyone else's (see Hosting and Online two-player below).

Data stored on your device

So you can continue next time, the Game uses the browser's local storage to save, on your device only: the weapons in your backpack, cleared levels and best times, which story scenes you've seen, the guards you draw and their names, and your volume and language settings. We cannot see this data and it is never sent anywhere. Settings → Erase all saves in the Game deletes your backpack, cleared levels, story progress and drawn guards; clearing the site data in your browser deletes everything, including the volume and language settings.

Hosting

The Game is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, when your browser downloads the Game, GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We cannot see these logs.

Online two-player

The network is used only when you choose Two Players → Open a room / Join a room: the host device's game picture and sound are sent directly to the joining device, and the joining device's button presses are sent back. To help the two devices find each other, the room number and connection details are exchanged through Supabase's realtime messaging, and public STUN services (Cloudflare, Google) are used to look up each device's network address; these services and the other device in the room can see your IP address. Nothing sent includes your name or any personal information, nothing is recorded or stored, and it ends when you leave the room.

Your drawings

Guards you draw stay on your device and are never uploaded. When you tap "Download", your device saves the image or hands it to the sharing option you choose.

External links

The Game only links to this site's support, privacy and licenses pages.

Children's privacy

The Game is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information, there is no data for a parent to consent to or ask us to delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
